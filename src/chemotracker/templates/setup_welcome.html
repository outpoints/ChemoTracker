Let's set up your medicines

ChemoTracker shows what to take today, when an as-needed medicine is OK again, and keeps a log of how you feel for your care team.

Helping someone else? Answer for them. Everything here can be changed later.

  1. Add who's using it
    You, and anyone who helps. Every entry shows who logged it.
  2. Pick your medicines
    Tick them in a list of common chemo and supportive medicines, or type one in.
  3. Copy each pharmacy label
    Strength, dose, directions, and which days of the cycle you take it.
  4. Set chemo day
    The date of Day 1. The schedule is worked out from there.
  5. Add your care team
    Phone numbers and your fever line, so they're one tap away.

Have your pharmacy bags or the clinic's medicine sheet nearby. It takes about 5 minutes.
